A single line of quiet, earned pride: an old fisherman's forty
thankless years of mending nets, landing at last through his
grandson's hands.

> Forty years he mended nets no one thanked him for; this morning
> his grandson hauled the season's biggest catch in on his
> grandfather's line.

Run `node main.ts`; it writes the line (140 characters, one line)
to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
